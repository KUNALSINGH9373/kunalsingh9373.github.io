Research Reports

The Open-Weight Paradox: How Model Release Policies Undermine Export Controls

Kunal Singh

US export controls on AI fail because they exempt publicly-released models. Anyone can download and fine-tune open-source models for military/harmful use at 1/1000th the original training cost. The report argues governments should focus on what's actually controllable: the expensive computer chips for training, transparency requirements, legal liability for developers, and defensive investments.
